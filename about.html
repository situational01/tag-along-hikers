Our story

More than reaching the top.

Tagalong Hikers is about making outdoor adventure easier to discover. We bring people together around organised hikes, practical preparation and the simple enjoyment of being outside.

From a relaxed forest walk to a challenging mountain route, every adventure starts with knowing the trail and respecting the people and places along it.

Our guides

People who know the trail.

Guides coordinate routes, brief the group, help keep the hike organised and communicate important information before and during the adventure.

Guide profiles, certifications and individual experience can be added here as the team grows.

Safety approach

Know before you go.

Trail difficulty, weather, distance, elevation and terrain all matter. Hikers should arrive prepared, stay with the group and follow the guide's instructions.
